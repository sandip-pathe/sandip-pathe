### About

I am a technical founder and full-stack AI engineer based in Mumbai, India. I grew up in rural Maharashtra, learned to code before college, and have spent the last few years building products across fintech compliance, legal workflows, research tooling, mental wellness, and AI automation.

My current focus is **Anaya**, a developer-first compliance platform for regulated fintech teams. It scans codebases and pull requests against RBI, SEBI, DPDPA, and PCI DSS controls, then turns violations into checks, annotations, and remediation guidance that engineering teams can act on.

I care most about systems where AI has to be useful, auditable, and grounded in real workflows.
